Anxiety and Mental Wellbeing

Anxiety is a common emotional response that can occur when a person feels worried, uncertain, or under threat. Occasional anxiety is a normal part of life.

Common experiences associated with anxiety can include excessive worry, feeling restless, difficulty relaxing, difficulty concentrating, irritability, and physical symptoms such as a faster heartbeat or muscle tension.

Helpful general coping strategies may include slow breathing, maintaining a regular sleep schedule, physical activity, reducing excessive caffeine, taking breaks, and talking with someone trusted.

Grounding techniques can help a person focus attention on the present moment. One simple approach is to notice things that can be seen, heard, felt, smelled, and tasted around them.

If anxiety is persistent, severe, or interfering with daily life, speaking with a qualified mental health professional can be helpful.

This information is educational and does not provide a diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.
